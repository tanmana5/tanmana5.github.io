# Interests

Things outside research that I care about.

<p class="section-title">Reading</p>

I would probably categorize my favourites as science fiction, YA, and the classics, in no particular order. *Ender's Game* is still one of my faves (read it when I was a tween and the emotions ran deep lol). Lately my shelf includes Elif Shafak's *There Are Rivers in the Sky* and Doris Lessing's *The Golden Notebook*. Other recent reads include *Remarkably Bright Creatures* (which I believe is becoming a movie) and R.F Kuang's *Katabasis*. The local library is one of my favourite hangouts, and one of my favourite pastimes is exploring cafés and dreaming about coffee beans being harvested from remote South American locations. Which reminds me *Journey to the River Sea* is also one book I must introduce to my daughter when she's older (banking on the fact that her reading tastes will somehow evolve to be similar to mine!).

<p class="section-title">Creating</p>

In my very limited free time, I also think about publishing the children's stories I've created or complete the YA novel idea I really want to flesh out. Hopefully someday.

<p class="section-title">Movement</p>

Nature walks are my reset button. The Westsong Walkway and West Bay Walkway near home are my regulars. Also totally rely on my mat Pilates practice to get me through the week!

<p class="section-title">Travels</p>

Of late, I've become enamored with visiting mangroves, rainforests and other fragile ecosystems of the world. Over the past two years I've actually made it to the *Sundarbans*, the Everglades, a part of SE Asia, and Cayman Islands in the Caribbean, getting to witness firsthand the magic of mangroves, tides, and even bioluminescence. Consider myself lucky, but hopefully only getting started.
