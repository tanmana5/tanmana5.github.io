# Interests

Things outside research that I care about.

<p class="section-title">Reading</p>

I would probably list my favourite genres as science fiction, YA, and the classics, in no particular order, although I enjoy reading across multiple. *Ender's Game* is still one of my faves (read it as a tween and the emotions ran deep lol). Lately my shelf includes Elif Shafak's *There Are Rivers in the Sky* and Doris Lessing's *The Golden Notebook*. Other recent reads include *Remarkably Bright Creatures*, *Katabasis* and *80-20: Development in an Unequal World*. The local library is undoubtedly one of my favourite hangouts, and one of my favourite pastimes is exploring cafés and dreaming about coffee beans being harvested from remote South American locations. Which reminds me *Journey to the River Sea* is also one book I must introduce to my daughter when she's older (banking on the fact that her reading tastes will somehow evolve to be similar to mine!).

<p class="section-title">Creating</p>

I created a group in LinkedIn called *AI for Good - Earth, Climate Change, Environment, Sustainability*. It has helped me forge meaningful, collaborative connections with people across the globe and given me insights into something I care about - the usage of technology in the right way, to benefit people equitably and take care of our planet.

Whenever I can, I also think about publishing the children's stories I've created or the YA novel I really want to flesh out. Hopefully someday.

<p class="section-title">Movement</p>

Nature walks are my reset button. The Westsong Walkway and West Bay Walkway near home are my regulars (will occasionally squeeze in a podcast). Also totally rely on my mat Pilates practice to get me through the week!

<p class="section-title">Travels</p>

Of late, I've become enamored with visiting mangroves, rainforests and other fragile ecosystems of the world. I don't think I can ever get over how truly beautiful the Earth is, and I can't stop marvelling at the visceral diversity of tropical landscapes. Over the past two years I've actually made it to the *Sundarbans* in India, the Everglades, Singapore and Cayman Islands in the Caribbean, getting to witness firsthand the magic of mangroves, tides, oceans and even [bioluminescence](https://caymanresident.com/bioluminescent-bay). Consider myself lucky, but hopefully only getting started.
