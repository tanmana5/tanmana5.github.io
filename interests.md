# Interests

Things outside research that I care about.

<p class="section-title">Reading</p>

I would probably categorize my favourites as science fiction, YA, and the classics, in no particular order. *Ender's Game* is still one of my faves (read it when I was 12, definietly used to read way more prolifically back then than I do now). Lately my shelf includes Elif Shafak's *There Are Rivers in the Sky* and Doris Lessing's "The Golden Notebook". Other recent reads include *Remarkably Bright Creatures* and *Katabasis*. The local library is one of my favourite hangouts, and I love exploring cafés and dreaming about coffee beans being harvested from remote South American locations ... which reminds me *Journey to the River Sea* is also one book I would absolutely love to introduce to my daughter when she's older.

<p class="section-title">Making</p>

In my very limited free time, I also dream of publishing my own children's stories or YA novel.

<p class="section-title">Outdoors</p>

Nature walks are my reset button. The Westsong Walkway and West Bay Walkway near home are my regulars.

<p class="section-title">Currently</p>

Of late, I've become enamored with visiting mangroves, rainforests and other fragile ecosystems. Over the past two years I've actually made it to the Sundarbans, the Everglades, SE Asia, and Cayman Islands in the Caribbean, getting to witness firsthand the magic of mangroves, tides, and even bioluminescence. I consider myself lucky, but hopefully only getting started.
